OUR STORY

A little café with
a lot of heart.

Sim & Sip was created with one simple idea — to make everyday coffee moments feel a little more special.

Inspired by Simme, a very loved four-legged companion, our café is built around warmth, comfort and the happiness found in life's simplest moments.

From freshly brewed coffee to homemade bakes, everything at Sim & Sip is made to be enjoyed slowly and shared with good company.
